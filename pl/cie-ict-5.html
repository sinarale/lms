This week, we start our new unit by exploring different types of bullying and what we can do to get help.

 

Our success criteria for the lesson are:

I can identify offensive and illegal online behavior, such as cyberbullying, that can harm others.

I understand the importance of reporting offensive and harmful online behavior to protect myself and others.

I understand the importance of privacy settings and knowing who I am interacting with online to ensure my safety during digital activities.

Your Job:

Create a poster about upsetting content.

In your poster:

1) Explain what upsetting content is

2) Give examples of upsetting content

3)Explain what people can do about upsetting content

4) Give examples of how people can refuse or report content

 

Green group: Create poster using sentences and images

Yellow group: Create poster using sentences and images and give specific examples of a type of content .

Red group: Create poster using detailed sentences, examples and links to websites people can use to report content i.e. roblox website or youtube
